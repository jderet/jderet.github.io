About

I studied classics at Sorbonne Université, following earlier studies at Sciences Po Bordeaux. Along the way, I spent a year in Rome at the Accademia Vivarium Novum and another in South Africa.

Today, I teach classics at a French secondary school. I am especially interested in how people come to understand an ancient language: through texts, reading and practice, rather than translation alone.

Research

THIVLVC

“THIVLVC: Retrieval Augmented Dependency Parsing for Latin”

Luc Pommeret, Thibault Wagret and Jules Deret.

LT4HALA 2026 / EvaLatin 2026, LREC 2026, Palma de Mallorca, 11 May 2026, pp. 219–225.

In 2026, I published this work with Luc Pommeret and Thibault Wagret. We asked whether previously annotated Latin sentences could help a language model correct an initial syntactic analysis. This is the principle behind THIVLVC.

Results and resources

In the EvaLatin 2026 evaluation, THIVLVC_2 achieved the best results on the poetry corpus and the best LAS without subtypes on the prose corpus. Compared with UDPipe, its CLAS score with subtypes improved by 17 points on poetry and 1.5 points on prose.

We also conducted a double-blind review of 300 disagreements with the reference corpus. Of the 167 unanimous decisions, 53.3% favoured THIVLVC.

The Greek Library

A digital library of Ancient Greek texts

I initiated this project with Luc Pommeret and Thibault Wagret.

The Greek Library provides free access to a large corpus of Ancient Greek texts, along with search and analysis tools. Hosted by Université Paris-Saclay, it is a free alternative to the Thesaurus Linguae Graecae.
